# Objectives

## Project Objective

The primary objective of the **Smart Event Management Automation System** is to automate the complete event management lifecycle, from participant registration to certificate distribution, using **n8n** and **Google Workspace** integrations.

---

## Specific Objectives

### 1. Automate Participant Registration

- Collect participant details through an online registration form.
- Store participant information in Google Sheets.
- Generate a unique Participant ID for every registration.

---

### 2. Generate QR Codes Automatically

- Create a unique QR code for each participant.
- Associate the QR code with the corresponding Participant ID.
- Store QR code information for attendance verification.

---

### 3. Streamline Attendance Tracking

- Verify participant attendance using QR codes.
- Automatically update attendance records.
- Prevent duplicate attendance entries.

---

### 4. Automate Certificate Generation

- Generate personalized certificates using a predefined Google Slides template.
- Replace participant-specific placeholders automatically.
- Export certificates as PDF documents.

---

### 5. Automate Certificate Distribution

- Upload generated certificates to Google Drive.
- Create shareable links.
- Email certificates automatically to participants.
- Update certificate delivery status in Google Sheets.

---

### 6. Improve Operational Efficiency

- Reduce manual effort in repetitive administrative tasks.
- Minimize human errors.
- Increase processing speed and reliability.
- Maintain a centralized participant database.

---

## Expected Outcomes

By implementing this automation system, event organizers can:

- Reduce manual administrative work.
- Improve accuracy of participant records.
- Accelerate certificate generation and distribution.
- Enhance participant experience through timely communication.
- Manage events more efficiently using an automated workflow.
