# Pain Points

## Existing Challenges

Traditional event management often relies on manual processes for registration, attendance tracking, and certificate distribution. These processes become increasingly inefficient as the number of participants grows.

---

## 1. Manual Registration Management

Participant information is manually collected and maintained, increasing the risk of data entry errors, duplicate records, and inconsistent information.

---

## 2. Time-Consuming QR Code Generation

Creating and distributing QR codes individually for every participant requires repetitive manual effort and becomes difficult to manage for large events.

---

## 3. Inefficient Attendance Tracking

Attendance verification is often performed manually, making it difficult to accurately record participant presence and increasing the possibility of duplicate or incorrect entries.

---

## 4. Manual Certificate Generation

Personalizing certificates for every participant requires editing names, participant IDs, and event details individually, making the process slow and error-prone.

---

## 5. Delayed Certificate Distribution

Sending certificates one by one through email consumes significant time and often leads to delays in delivery.

---

## 6. Lack of Centralized Data Management

Participant records, attendance details, and certificate status are often stored across multiple platforms, making tracking and reporting difficult.

---

## 7. Repetitive Administrative Work

Event organizers spend considerable time performing repetitive tasks instead of focusing on event coordination and participant engagement.

---

## Solution Provided

The Smart Event Management Automation System addresses these challenges by automating participant registration, QR code generation, attendance tracking, certificate generation, cloud storage, and email distribution using n8n workflows integrated with Google Workspace services.
